FakeGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

FakeGuard helps you judge whether pictures and videos are genuine, edited, or AI-generated. This page explains what information FakeGuard uses and what happens to it.

The short version

Most of FakeGuard's checks run on your device and send nothing anywhere. FakeGuard also offers optional online checks, which compare what you are viewing with fact-checks and earlier appearances on the web. Online checks are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off again at any time.

What stays on your device

Most of FakeGuard's analysis runs locally. The pictures and videos you check are not uploaded. FakeGuard keeps a small local record of each check (a short fingerprint and the result) so it does not repeat work; older entries are deleted automatically.

What is sent when online checks are on

Only if you turn online checks on, FakeGuard may send the web address of the content you are viewing, and any nearby caption or claim text, to third-party fact-checking and image-search services. It may also visit a few public web pages related to the content to look for an original copy. These visits work like any web visit, so those sites can see standard connection details such as your internet address. The picture or video file itself is never uploaded, and this information is used only to produce your result.

Your choices

When you first open FakeGuard it asks whether to turn on online checks, and nothing is sent until you say yes. You can change your mind at any time in settings. With online checks off, you still get results from all of FakeGuard's other checks.

How long information is kept

Information sent for online checks is used only to produce your result. Where a service allows it, we ask the service to delete that information as soon as the result has been received. Third-party services may keep limited records under their own policies. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or read it for any other purpose.

Compliance

The use of information received from your browser by FakeGuard adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
